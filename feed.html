Your Questions, Answered

Everything you need to know about our feed and care services.

What makes HASC feed Halal?
Our feed is composed strictly of plant-based ingredients (grains, forage) and minerals. We ensure no animal by-products, blood meal, or haram additives are ever used in the production process.
How do your veterinary services work?
You can book a virtual consultation or a physical visit through our portal. Our licensed vets will assess your animal via video call or on-site visit and prescribe necessary treatments.
Can I customize my feed subscription?
Yes! Our nutritionist can create a custom blend based on your herd size and breed. Contact support to set up a custom plan.

Need Tailored Feed & Care Advice?

Our experts are ready to help you choose the best for your livestock. Contact us today for personalized plans.
